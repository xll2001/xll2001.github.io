Longlong Xu

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Dan Pei in NetMan Lab. My research lies at the intersection of LLM post-training, multimodal LLM alignment, reasoning, and their applications to long-sequence numerical data and AIOps.

My recent work focuses on LLM post-training and reasoning in practical, data-rich settings: aligning model behavior with user intent, improving fine-grained localization over long numerical sequences through multimodal perception and tool use, and building reliable evaluation protocols for domain-specific LLMs.

I received my B.E. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University in 2023. I have held algorithm research internships at ByteDance, Huawei, ZTE, eBay, and RealAI, producing two first-author ICML 2026 papers and two first-author CCF-A submissions under review.
